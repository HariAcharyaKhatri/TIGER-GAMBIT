📖 How to Play

Master the ancient game of Bagh-Chal

🐅 The Tigers (4)

Tigers start at the four corners. They move along lines to adjacent empty spots and can capture goats by jumping over them to an empty space beyond — like in checkers.

🐐 The Goats (20)

Goats are placed one by one on any empty spot during the Placement Phase. Once all 20 are placed, goats may move one step along any line to an adjacent empty spot.

🎯 Winning Conditions

  • Tigers win by capturing 5 goats.
  • Goats win by trapping all 4 tigers so none can move.
  • A stalemate where no legal moves exist awards goats the win.

💡 Strategy Tips

  • As goats: control the center and block tiger escape routes.
  • As tigers: stay mobile, avoid corners, and hunt isolated goats.
  • Placement phase is crucial — spread goats to limit tiger movement early.
  • Watch for capture chains — tigers can pounce multiple times!

🛤️ The Board

The 5×5 grid has 25 intersection points connected by a network of lines — including diagonals and special corner connections. All pieces sit on intersections and move along these lines.

📜 History

Bagh-Chal (meaning "Tiger's Move") is a traditional Nepali board game dating back centuries. It's played across Nepal and parts of India, beloved for its asymmetric yet deeply balanced gameplay.
